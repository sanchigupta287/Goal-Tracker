# Problem Statement: Goal & Habit Tracker

## Problem Statement
Students often struggle to stay consistent with their daily study habits, fitness routines, and long-term personal goals due to a lack of simple, structured tracking tools.

## Target Users
First-year college students, self-learners, and individuals seeking a lightweight tool to monitor personal targets.

## High-Level Features
1. **Goal Management (CRUD):** Create new goals with target dates, view existing goals, update progress, and delete obsolete goals.
2. **Progress Logging:** Record completion progress percentage and maintain track of active tasks.
3. **Analytics Summary:** View total completed goals, average completion rate, and goal statistics.
